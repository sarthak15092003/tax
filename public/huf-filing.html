💡 What Is HUF? Why Should You Create One?

HUF (Hindu Undivided Family) is a separate legal entity for income tax purposes. It gets its own PAN, files its own ITR, and enjoys the full slate of tax deductions independently from you as an individual.

Example Tax Saving:
Individual tax (₹20L income after deductions): ~₹3.1L
After HUF: Transfer ₹8L to HUF → HUF pays ~₹52K → You pay ~₹1.8L
Total Tax Saved: ~₹1.28L per year! 🎉
  • ✅HUF can own property, invest in stocks, mutual funds
  • ✅HUF can have its own FD, NPS, PPF, LIC policy
  • ✅HUF can run a business and show business income
  • ✅All slab rates, rebates apply to HUF just like individuals
  • ✅Available to Hindus, Sikhs, Jains, Buddhists

🚀 How To Create HUF — 3 Steps

Step 1: HUF Deed
Draft a notarized HUF Deed declaring Karta and members. TaxBuddy prepares this for you.
Step 2: PAN Application
Apply for HUF PAN using Form 49A. PAN is issued in the name "Rajesh Kumar HUF". Received within 7-10 days.
Step 3: Open HUF Bank Account
Open a bank account in HUF name. Start routing family income (gifts, ancestral property income, FD interest) through HUF.
⚠️ Note: HUF cannot be created solely for tax savings purposes. It should have legitimate coparceners (family members). Consult a CA before creating HUF.
